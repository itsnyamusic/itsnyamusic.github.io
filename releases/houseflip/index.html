Houseflip is a single by Nya, released on October 16, 2026.

Cyber rap on a tech house beat. The track is a mashup that flips four older songs into one: Anschluss, Leider, Letzte Nacht and 3 Flaschen Wein.

First performed live at DIGITALGODS SHOW, 10 October 2026, where it closed Nya's set.

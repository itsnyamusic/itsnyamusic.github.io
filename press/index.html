LoudHERMedia - 19 August 2026 - Fallon Oneill
Nya Confronts the Fears That Follow Transformation on “Duality2”
The first piece of writing to sit with “Duality2” as the closing statement of NYAVERSE rather than treat it as one more single. It reads the track as a companion to the 2021 EP Duality, returned to with a more mature perspective, and refuses the easy version of the story: growth is messy and incomplete, and transformation does not arrive as a clean slate. The argument at the centre of it is that changing an appearance, an identity or an artistic direction does not automatically leave the old fears behind, and the “new name, new face, new everything” line is heard as a vulnerable admission rather than a victory lap. On the production it credits the full experimental range of hyperpop, glitch-heavy work set against shimmering synths, distorted textures and unpredictable rhythmic shifts, and hears the fragmentation as deliberate: every glitch and abrupt transition restates the idea that identity itself is rarely stable. A close read rather than a roundup blurb, written by hand and accurate about Nya throughout.

Hertener Allgemeine - 12 August 2026 - André Przybyl
Herten musician “Nya” Jahn releases debut album
A full interview in print with the local paper in Herten: seven years of music as a hobby, then the decision to go all in, and NYAVERSE as a self-portrait of being in your early twenties, 13 tracks that are self-written and self-mixed. Also covers the response to the album, called “schockierend gut”, songs that run from “Sweet Monster Energy” as a love song to “Denk Wieder an Dich” out of an earlier breakup, the nerves before the first live show in Düsseldorf, and the home-town concert being planned in Herten.

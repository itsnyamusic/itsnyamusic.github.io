DIGITALGODS SHOW

live - 10.10.2026 - illusion event club, herten
Recap

DIGITALGODS SHOW took place on Saturday, 10 October 2026 at Illusion Event Club in Herten. Nya organized the night and headlined it.

Seven acts played back to back across deutschrap, cloud rap, cyber-rap and hyperpop: Jessil, lylyth, Max Rolls, Naeyiwu, Nya, OhCris! and MTeTu. Every act on the final bill played, and the night ran smoothly.

Nya's set was the first time songs from NYAVERSE were played live. It also included the then unreleased Cyber-Rap, out as a single on 13.11.2026, and closed with Houseflip, which had its live debut there six days before its release as a single.
